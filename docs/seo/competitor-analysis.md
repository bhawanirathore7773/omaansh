## Patterns to compete against

1. **Specificity:** competitors frequently use manufacturer + product/material + Jaipur in titles and headings.
2. **Commercial details:** MOQ, size, material, specifications and quotation/contact paths help buyers qualify suppliers.
3. **Entity trust:** business address, phone, seller profile and real product imagery are prominent.
4. **Buyer pathways:** wholesale/distributor, promotional branding and custom-order enquiries are distinct from catalogue browsing.
5. **Proof:** directories and some supplier pages display ratings, testimonials, videos or seller verification. OSIRA must not imitate these with fabricated proof.

## OSIRA opportunity

- Make the homepage the clear Jaipur manufacturer landing page, with an exact, natural H1 and unique useful copy.
- Surface the supplied MOQ rules accurately where relevant: 100 units for stock designs, 100 units for logo-customised orders, and 5,000 units for fully custom designs. Confirm these remain current before publishing them on transactional pages.
- Explain the difference between stock selection, logo branding and fully custom/OEM development.
- Show real, current product/factory photography and accurate business identity (OSIRA brand; Omaansh Industries legal business).
- Make enquiry CTAs specific to bulk orders, corporate gifting and custom/OEM requests.
- Keep address/phone/email consistent across the site and business listings.
- Build category pages around real inventory and buyer questions, not thin keyword variants.
- Earn third-party proof organically; do not add fake ratings, testimonials, client names, certifications or marketplace badges.
