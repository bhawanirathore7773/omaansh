## Cannibalisation rules

- Homepage owns **“wall clock manufacturer in Jaipur.”**
- Jaipur city page should target **bulk/business supply in Jaipur**, with distinct copy and internal links.
- Do not publish multiple pages with the same title, H1 and search intent.
- Product filters and query-string variants should be canonicalised to the clean catalogue URL unless a query page has a deliberate, useful indexation strategy.
- Do not create city pages for places without distinct service relevance and original local information.
