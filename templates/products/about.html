OUR STORY

A supplier relationship should make buying easier.

{{ site.business_name }} was established in Jaipur in 2021 with a clear ambition: to design and manufacture thoughtfully crafted wall clocks and décor products that bring together timeless aesthetics, reliable quality and modern innovation.

The business operates from a fully in-house facility of approximately 4,000 sq. ft. with a stated production capability of up to 1 lakh clocks per month. The focus is on continuous improvement in design, manufacturing standards and customer experience.

OSIRA aims to serve homes, workplaces and commercial spaces across India and, over time, build a globally admired brand known for creativity, reliability and craftsmanship.
